Frequently Asked Questions

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided items are unworn and in original packaging. This is a demo site, so returns are illustrative only.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping typically takes 3–5 business days. Orders over $75 ship free; expedited options are shown at checkout.

Do you ship internationally?

We currently ship within the United States and Canada. International shipping is on our roadmap for a future release.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking link by email. In this demo, checkout and order tracking are for illustration only.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Orders can be modified within one hour of placing them by contacting support directly — after that, our fulfillment team begins processing.
